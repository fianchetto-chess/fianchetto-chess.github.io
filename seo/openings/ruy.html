Ruy Lopez (Spanish Game)

The most popular answer to 1...e5 among top players. White pressures the c6 knight, the defender of e5.

The main line

  1. 1. e4 — Centre.
  2. e5 — Symmetrical reply.
  3. 2. Nf3 — Attacking e5.
  4. Nc6 — Defending.
  5. 3. Bb5 — The Spanish bishop pressures the knight that defends e5.
  6. a6 — Morphy Defence: Black questions the bishop at once.
  7. 4. Ba4 — The bishop retreats, keeping the pin for later.
  8. Nf6 — Developing with an attack on e4.
  9. 5. O-O — White castles: taking on e4 is still risky for Black.
  10. Be7 — Closed Ruy Lopez: Black prepares to castle.
  11. 6. Re1 — The rook defends e4.
  12. b5 — Chasing the bishop.
  13. 7. Bb3 — The bishop eyes f7 again.
  14. d6 — Supporting e5.
  15. 8. c3 — Preparing d4 and a retreat square on c2.
  16. O-O — The main position of the Closed Ruy Lopez.
