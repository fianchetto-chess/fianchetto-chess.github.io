Простая система для белых: одна и та же расстановка почти против всего.

London System

A simple system for White: almost the same setup against everything.

The main line

  1. 1. d4 — Centre.
  2. d5 — Reply.
  3. 2. Bf4 — The key idea: the bishop comes out before e3.
  4. Nf6 — Development.
  5. 3. e3 — Pawn chain.
  6. c5 — Black hits the centre.
  7. 4. c3 — Supporting d4 — the c3-d4-e3 "triangle".
  8. Nc6 — Pressure on d4.
  9. 5. Nd2 — The knight heads to f3 via d2, staying flexible.
  10. e6 — Black opens the f8 bishop.
  11. 6. Ngf3 — The second knight joins.
  12. Bd6 — Black offers a bishop trade.
  13. 7. Bg3 — Keeping the bishop.
  14. O-O — Castling.
  15. 8. Bd3 — The typical London setup.
