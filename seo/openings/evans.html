Evans Gambit

An Italian Game gambit: White gives the b-pawn to gain time and build a big centre.

  6. Bc5 — Giuoco Piano.

  8. Bxb4 — Black accepts.

  11. 6. d4 — A strong centre.
  12. exd4 — Black takes again.
  13. 7. O-O — White develops fast — a big lead in development for two pawns.
