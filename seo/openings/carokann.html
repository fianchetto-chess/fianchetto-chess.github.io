Caro-Kann Defence

A solid defence: Black develops the c8 bishop before playing ...e6 and gets a sturdy position.

The main line

  1. 1. e4 — Centre.
  2. c6 — Caro-Kann: preparing ...d5 supported by a pawn.
  3. 2. d4 — White takes the centre.
  4. d5 — Striking at e4.
  5. 3. Nc3 — Defending.
  6. dxe4 — Central exchange.
  7. 4. Nxe4 — Knight in the centre.
  8. Bf5 — The key idea: the bishop gets outside the pawn chain before ...e6.
  9. 5. Ng3 — Attacking the bishop.
  10. Bg6 — The bishop retreats but stays active.
  11. 6. h4 — White threatens h5 to trap the bishop.
  12. h6 — Giving the bishop a retreat on h7.
  13. 7. Nf3 — Development.
  14. Nd7 — Black controls e5. A solid position.
