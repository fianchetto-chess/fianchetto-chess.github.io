Two Knights & Fried Liver

A famous trap line: if Black recaptures on d5 with the knight, White sacrifices on f7.

The main line

  1. 1. e4 — Centre.
  2. e5 — Reply.
  3. 2. Nf3 — Attack on e5.
  4. Nc6 — Defence.
  5. 3. Bc4 — The bishop eyes f7.
  6. Nf6 — Two Knights Defence — a counterattack on e4.
  7. 4. Ng5 — Attacking f7 twice.
  8. d5 — Blocking the bishop.
  9. 5. exd5 — Taking.
  10. Nxd5 — A mistake! (Correct is ...Na5.)
  11. 6. Nxf7 — The Fried Liver: a knight sacrifice!
  12. Kxf7 — The king is forced out.
  13. 7. Qf3+ — Attack on the king and the d5 knight.
  14. Ke6 — The only way to hold the knight.
  15. 8. Nc3 — Bringing more pieces against the king in the centre.
