The main line

  1. 1. e4 — Centre.
  2. e5 — Central reply.
  3. 2. Nf3 — Attacking e5.
  4. Nc6 — Defending.
  5. 3. d4 — The Scotch: an immediate central strike.
  6. exd4 — Black takes — otherwise e5 is lost.
  7. 4. Nxd4 — White regains the pawn with a knight in the centre.
  8. Nf6 — Attacking e4.
  9. 5. Nxc6 — Mieses Variation: trading knights.
  10. bxc6 — Doubled pawns, but the b-file opens.
  11. 6. e5 — Kicking the f6 knight.
  12. Qe7 — Pinning the e5 pawn to the king!
  13. 7. Qe2 — Defending the pawn.
  14. Nd5 — The knight takes the centre.
  15. 8. c4 — Chasing the knight. A sharp position with chances for both sides.
